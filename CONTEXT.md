# Tideline Six

A coastal sports meet across six disciplines.

## Language

**Event**: One of six sports, with its own controls and scoring.

**Run**: One timed play of an event. Flying disc instead ends after three throws.

**Practice**: A standalone run used to learn an event.

**Meet**: Six event runs played in order, with a combined medal-point result.

**Bail**: A failed maneuver or collision that consumes one of a run's falls.

**Record**: The best competition score earned in an event on this browser.

**Medal points**: Five for gold, three for silver, one for bronze; awarded against published event targets.
